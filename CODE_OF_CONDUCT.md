# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want the Oxide community to be welcoming, respectful and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberately inflammatory remarks, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all oxide-hq spaces — repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact a member of the
[@oxide-hq/maintainers](https://github.com/orgs/oxide-hq/teams/maintainers) team privately, or use
GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that violate this code, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is
harmful. Responses are proportionate: a private warning for a first, minor issue; a ban for
sustained or severe harassment.
