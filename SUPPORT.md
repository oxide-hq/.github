# Getting help

- **Questions, ideas and show-and-tell:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/oxide-hq/oxide-framework/discussions).
- **Bugs and feature requests:** [open an issue](https://github.com/oxide-hq/oxide-framework/issues/new/choose)
  using one of the templates.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) — never a public issue.

Oxide is maintained by volunteers; please be patient and include a minimal reproduction when you
ask about a problem.
